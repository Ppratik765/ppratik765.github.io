01 // About Me

Background &
Focus

I'm Priyanshu — third-year AI & Data Science undergrad at Gati Shakti Vishwavidyalaya, with an AI engineering internship at BISAG-N behind me. Most of what I build starts from the same itch: I want to see the system actually run, not just describe how it should work.

That's meant quantizing PyTorch models down to run offline on a phone, and building multi-agent reinforcement-learning simulators that argue with each other over supply chain routing. Somewhere in between, I taught myself GLSL because a flight simulator needed better clouds — that instinct to go one layer deeper is basically my whole personality at this point.

I keep a decent head for math and data structures — 8.80 CGPA, five semesters in — and I'm chipping away at GATE 2027 (Data Science & AI) between projects. On the side, I'm 160+ problems deep on LeetCode, mostly because stopping felt worse than continuing.

Before university: Year 11 at Soar Valley College, England (seven Grade 9s at GCSE), then DPS Harni for the rest — 435/500 in Class 12, and a JEE Main run that landed at 96.6 percentile.
