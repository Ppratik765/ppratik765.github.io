Traffic Digital Twin

Domain Intelligent Transportation Systems / Computer Vision
Core Stack PyTorch, YOLOv8x, ByteTrack, SegFormer, OpenCV, Three.js, Vite
Architecture Decoupled perception engine (Colab) and 3D web twin
View Code on GitHub
YOLOv8x FP16 detection at 1280 px
60 frame ByteTrack occlusion buffer
240 m road orthophoto span, SegFormer-generated
60+ FPS interpolated 3D playback

Purpose & Necessity

Traffic cameras already cover most junctions, but their footage is flat and hard to analyse at a glance. This project rebuilds a junction as a metric 3D scene from a single ordinary CCTV camera, so vehicle positions, speeds and headings can be inspected interactively instead of eyeballed from video.

Key Technical Implementation

A two-tier system. The perception engine runs YOLOv8x at 1280 px in FP16 with a low confidence threshold to catch small distant vehicles, tracks them with ByteTrack using a 60-frame buffer to survive occlusions, and projects each vehicle's ground-contact point through a 4-point homography into metres.

Trajectories are cleaned with boundary-aware Savitzky-Golay filtering and median despiking, then exported with a road texture that SegFormer segments out of a 50-frame median background. The Three.js front end plays synchronised video and 3D side by side, interpolating motion with LERP/SLERP and letting you click any vehicle to read its ID, class, speed and heading.
