Ambulance Corridor

Domain Intelligent Transportation Systems / Multimodal Deep Learning
Core Stack YOLOv8, PyTorch, torchaudio, librosa, Vanilla JS, Canvas, SVG, Vite
Fusion Rule Late Bayesian fusion of vision and audio probabilities
View Code on GitHub
0.75 fused probability that triggers preemption
64-band log-Mel spectrograms at 16 kHz
5-layer 2D CNN siren classifier
2 sensors fused: camera and microphone

Purpose & Necessity

An ambulance stuck at a red light loses minutes it does not have. Cameras alone miss vehicles that are occluded or far away, and microphones alone confuse sirens with traffic noise. Fusing both signals gives a more reliable trigger for clearing the road ahead.

Key Technical Implementation

A Colab pipeline fine-tunes YOLOv8n on an emergency-vehicle dataset and trains a 5-layer 2D CNN on 64-band log-Mel spectrograms to separate sirens from ambient noise. The two confidences are combined as P = 1 - (1 - P_vision)(1 - P_audio), and preemption fires when the fused probability reaches 0.75.

The command-centre front end is plain JavaScript with Canvas and SVG: a video pane with live bounding boxes, three confidence gauges, a Web Audio waveform and spectrogram, and a four-way intersection that turns conflicting lanes red and locks the ambulance approach green, all kept in sync when you scrub the timeline.
