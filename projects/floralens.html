FloraLens

Domain Agritech / Edge AI / Android Development
Core Stack Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, TensorFlow Lite (NNAPI), PyTorch Lightning, timm
Privacy Fully on-device inference, no network required
View Code on GitHub
99.5% reported diagnostic accuracy
38 plant disease classes
384 px MobileNetV4-Conv-Large input
Offline fully on-device inference via NNAPI

Purpose & Necessity

Plant diseases are easiest to treat when spotted early, but farmers and gardeners often have no reliable connection or expert to ask. FloraLens puts the diagnosis on the phone itself, so it works in a field with no signal and no photo ever leaves the device.

Key Technical Implementation

A MobileNetV4-Conv-Large model at 384 px was trained on a 38-class plant disease dataset with PyTorch Lightning and timm, using mixed precision and heavy augmentation (colour jitter, rotations, RandomErasing) to survive real-world lighting. It was compiled to TensorFlow Lite with signatures tuned for NNAPI acceleration on Android.

Two safeguards keep results honest: an HSV pre-filter rejects frames with under 12.5% organic pixels so sofas and fabrics are not diagnosed, and a 1.5x bias toward healthy classes means a disease is only reported on strong evidence. The Compose app adds a spline timeline of scan history, physics-based animation and PDF report export.
